# AskMentor - 30 Second Launch Video Script

**Target Platforms:** LinkedIn, Instagram (Reels / Feed)
**Goal:** Introduce AskMentor, highlight its unique document-to-mentor capability, showcase the UI, and drive early adoption.
**Tone:** Professional, energetic, and cutting-edge.

---

| Time | Visual / Video Clip | Audio / Voiceover (VO) & Sound Effects (SFX) |

## 🛠️ Recommended AI Tools for Production

To make this video look highly professional while saving time, here is the recommended tech stack for production:

### 1. Screen Recording (Crucial for UI showcase)
*   **Screen Studio (macOS) / FocuSee (Windows):** These are absolute game-changers for SaaS videos. They automatically add beautiful backgrounds, smooth cursor tracking, and professional zoom-ins on the UI without manual keyframing. This will make the "Screen Recording" sections look premium.

### 2. Voice Generation (Voiceover)
*   **ElevenLabs:** Since AskMentor already uses ElevenLabs in its backend, you should use it for the promotional video's voiceover! Use a confident, engaging voice (like "Marcus" or "Adam"). It sounds 100% human and maintains brand consistency.

### 3. Video Editing & Captioning
*   **CapCut (Desktop/Pro):** The best tool for assembling the clips, adding the background music, and generating dynamic, animated captions (which are essential for Instagram and LinkedIn where many watch on mute).
*   **OpusClip:** If you record a longer demo video (e.g., 2 minutes) and want an AI to automatically trim it down into the best 30-second vertical hooks, OpusClip is fantastic.

### 4. B-Roll & Visual Assets
*   **Runway Gen-2 / Midjourney:** If you need that initial "brain lighting up" graphic or a custom frustrated student image for the first 5 seconds, use these to generate high-quality B-roll instead of using generic stock footage. 
*   **Canva:** Use for designing the final Call-to-Action end screen with your logo.

### 📝 Execution Tips:
*   **Aspect Ratio:** Export two versions. One **9:16** (Vertical) for Instagram Reels and one **4:5** or **1:1** (Square) for the LinkedIn feed for optimal screen real estate.
*   **Pacing:** Keep the cuts fast. A 30-second ad should have visual changes every 2-3 seconds to retain attention.
